    ★ THE RAREST COIN IN THE REALM · THE FARE FOR THE CROSSING ★

    ONE-OF-ONE

    Duel for the Genesis Coin

    A genesis coin, minted once and never again. Cross the night ferry and beat Marlow the Ferryman
    in a duel of crypto coins, spells and nerve — pay the fare with the Genesis Coin, or lose it to him.

    HOW TO PLAY — Both duelists have 8000 Life. Each turn draw 1 card and gain mana.
    Summon coins (cost = stars), attack rival coins or directly. Protocols combine with coins.
    Forge ▲ mints fire, Tide ● flows, Gale ◆ strikes fast, Stone ■ endures. Rarities run Common to
    Legendary — and the one Mythic Genesis Coin. First to 0 Life loses. The ferry waits for no one.
