    Duel for the Genesis Coin

    A genesis coin, minted once and never again. Beat Vex the Collector
    in a duel of crypto coins, spells and nerve — and the Genesis Coin is yours.

    Legendary — and the one Mythic Genesis Coin. First to 0 Life loses.
